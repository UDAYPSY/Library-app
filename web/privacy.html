TrackMyLibrary — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 24, 2026 | DPDP Act 2023 Compliant

Welcome to TrackMyLibrary ("we", "our", or "us"). We provide digital seat and fee management solutions for self-study reading rooms and library operators.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

We collect only the minimum required information to facilitate student registration, shift seat allotment, and fee collection:

  • Student identification (Name, Mobile Number, Father's Name, Preparation Exam).
  • Government ID (Aadhaar number if voluntarily provided by library operator for security verification).
  • Seat assignment, shift subscription history, and payment transactions.

2. Data Security & Storage

All sensitive student data and library records are stored with AES-256 bit encryption at rest and in transit. Student data is strictly isolated within tenant namespaces. We do not sell or monetize personal student or operator data.

3. Data Principal Rights (DPDP Act 2023)

Students and library owners have the right to review, update, export, or request deletion of their records at any time directly through the application's profile management or by contacting our Data Protection Officer.
